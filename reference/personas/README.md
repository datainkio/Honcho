# People

Personas for people who touch an application, plus the automated systems that sit between the application and a human reader. Each note describes who the reader is, what they decide, and what moves them forward or rules the candidate out.

For more insight into the roles the people and systems play in the application lifecycle, explore the [[reference/journeys/README|user journeys and task flows]].

## Index

### 🧑 People

| Note                                              | Influence | Summary                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [🧑 Recruiter](🧑%20Recruiter.md)                 | screens   | First to see the application; checks it against the hiring manager's brief   |
| [🧑 Hiring Manager](🧑%20Hiring%20Manager.md)     | decides   | Owns the role; scans for a clear match, seniority, and personal contribution |
| [🧑 Team Lead](🧑%20Team%20Lead.md)               | approves  | Product lead or director; looks for outcomes and judgment under ambiguity    |

| [🧑 Applicant](<🧑 Applicant.md>)                 | applies   | Job applicant; looks to move applications into “accepted” status             |
