# Digital Designer

A peer who would work alongside the candidate. Asked for an opinion, usually between other tasks.

## Decides
"Is the craft real, and would I want to work with this person?"
## Attention
A few minutes. Goes straight to the work.
## Looks for
Process behind the polish. 
Reasons for decisions. 
Care in the portfolio itself.
## Rules out
Finished screens with no thinking shown. 
A vague "we" that hides the candidate's role.
## Moves it forward by
Telling the hiring manager "worth talking to."
