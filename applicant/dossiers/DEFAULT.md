# <Your Name> — Professional Profile

Copy this file to `applicant/dossiers/<profile>.md`, point your profile's `dossier` at it, and fill it in. It is the **only** source of facts the skills may use: a claim missing here can't appear in a résumé or letter. Keep the numbered `## N.` headings as they are. The skills read the profile by section number (for example §10–§11), so a renamed or renumbered heading breaks them. Mark anything unverified as such.

## 1. At a glance

Name, location, email, phone, portfolio and LinkedIn URLs. Seniority band, primary target titles, sweet spot.

## 2. Positioning statements

Two or three one-paragraph positioning statements, one per target role family, plus a cover-letter opener.

## 3. Career arc — the through-line

The story that connects your roles.

## 4. Employment history

Reverse-chronological: employer, title as it must be written, dates, scope, key work.

## 5. Disciplines & skills

Grouped skills, methods and tools, with proficiency where it matters.

## 6. Industries & domains

Each domain with the evidence a letter should lead with.

## 7. Signature themes — the intellectual fingerprint

The recurring ideas that make your work recognisably yours.

## 8. Values & character

## 9. How you work

Process, collaboration, leadership style.

## 10. Evidence & impact (attributable, strongest first)

Results you can attribute to your own work, with figures and sources.

## 11. Impact claims — honest-use guide

A table: the claim as you've written it before, what it actually measures, and how to frame it defensibly.

## 12. Education & credentials

Each degree with its status (conferred or in progress). Certifications.

## 13. Job-search targeting

Titles, seniority, locations, deal-breakers.

## 14. Voice & language kit

Words and phrases you use, words to avoid, tone.

## 15. Open items / to verify

Anything a draft must not state until confirmed.

## 16. Sources

Where the facts above come from.

## 17. Application form defaults

Standard answers for application forms: name, contact, work authorisation, links.
