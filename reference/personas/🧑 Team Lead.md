# Team 
A product lead or director, beside or above the role. Sees a shortlist, not the full pool.
## Decides
"Will this person make the product and the team better?"
## Attention
More time per candidate, fewer candidates.
## Looks for
- Outcomes. 
- Judgment under ambiguity. 
- How disagreements were resolved.
## Rules out
- Craft with no results.
- No evidence of working across disciplines.
## Moves it forward by
Approving the hire, or not blocking it.
