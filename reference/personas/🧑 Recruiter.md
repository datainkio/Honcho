# Recruiter
The first person to see the application. Usually not a designer.
Working many roles at once from the hiring manager's brief.
## Decides
"Does this person meet the brief well enough to show the hiring manager?"
## Attention
Under a minute on the resume. Opens the portfolio only if the resume passes.
## Looks for
Titles and years that match the posting.
The posting's own words.
Location, work authorization, and salary fit.
A portfolio link that works.
## Rules out
A missing stated requirement.
A resume that is hard to scan.
A career they can't sum up in one sentence.
## Moves it forward by
Adding the candidate to the shortlist, often after a short phone screen.
