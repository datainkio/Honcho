# Cover Letter Template

Copy this file to `applicant/cover letters/<profile>.md`, point your profile's `cover letter template` at it, and replace every <placeholder> with your own text.

A cover letter is an opportunity to establish a human connection within an anonymous process. The goal of this document is to provide direction on integrating insights from connections between my background and the job as described in the post. Its structure follows the same structure as the rendered letter.

## Header

1. **<Your Name>**
2. **contact:** <email> | <phone>
3. **portfolio:** <portfolio URL>

## Date

Display the date on which the cover letter was rendered

1. **MMMM D, YYYY**

## Address

If the post author is identified:

1. First and last name of the post author
2. _Post Author's Title_
3. {{ Organization }}

If the post author is not identified, display nothing.

## Dear...

If the name of the post author is available: "Dear " + First name of the post author
Otherwise: "Dear " + {{ Organization }}

## Introduction

<A paragraph that holds for every letter: who you are, what you have done that matters most, and why you do this work. Every claim must trace to your dossier.>

<One sentence on the kind of role you are looking for.>

## Reflection

{{ Organization }} is looking for a {{ job title }} to {{ a concise, short description of the goals for the role }}.

## Context

State the organization's goals as evidenced by the job description. The tactic here is to demonstrate familiarity with the particular organization, thereby communicating genuine interest in the role:

1. Does the role focus on a product or a service?
2. What audiences does the product or service serve?
