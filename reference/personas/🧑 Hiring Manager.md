# Hiring Manager

Owns the open role and would manage the candidate.
Reviewing many applications against one posting.

## Decides

"Can this person do this job at this level?"

## Attention

The shortest of the four. Scans first, reads only if the scan holds up.

## Looks for

A clear match to the posting.
Seniority.
What the candidate personally did on each project.

## Rules out

Having to hunt for relevance. Unclear role or level.
Generic, vague, or buzzword-heavy content that is characteristic of poorly edited AI output.

## Moves it forward by

Scheduling a first conversation.
