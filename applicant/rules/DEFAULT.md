# Truth rules

Copy this file to `applicant/rules/<profile>.md`, point your profile's `rules` at it, and replace each example with your own rule. Every résumé and cover letter obeys these rules, whatever the posting asks for. They distil your dossier (the profile's `dossier`), which holds the evidence behind each. If this file and the dossier disagree, the dossier wins: fix this file.

Write a rule for each trap a draft could fall into. Delete a category that doesn't apply to you.

## Rules

1. **Program-scale numbers are context, never personal outcomes.** List each large number from your career that describes a program, campaign or market rather than your own work, and say what it is. Example: '$2B is the agency's annual budget, not a result of the redesign.'
2. **Preferred proof.** The two or three results you can attribute to your own work, with their exact figures.
3. **Education and credentials.** The status of each degree and credential. Example: 'The M.S. is in progress: never state it as conferred.'
4. **No invented or estimated metrics.** Name any projects whose outcomes are unmeasured, so a draft treats them as design intent.
5. **Recognition.** Awards that are easy to misattribute or double-count, and the correct attribution.
6. **Titles.** Any title that must be written a particular way, or never used.
7. **Current status.** What you do now and since when; which past roles are past tense; any gap and how (or whether) to address it.
